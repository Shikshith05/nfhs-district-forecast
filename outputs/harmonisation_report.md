## What is needed to recover more districts

1. **Parents of new NFHS-6 districts and the territory changes of flagged districts** (`data/lineage/lineage_supplied.csv`, transition `5to6`), from the Local Government Directory (lgdirectory.gov.in, 'Download Only Modifications', districts, 2019-2024) or state gazette notifications.
2. **Populations** of every NFHS-5 and NFHS-6 district in a changed component (`data/lineage/population.csv`), e.g. Census 2011 populations re-aggregated to current boundaries via sub-districts. Without them, splits are scored by inheritance (flagged, extra variance).
3. **NFHS-4 district values on Census-2011 boundaries** (`data/lineage/nfhs4_census2011_values.csv`, from the IIPS NFHS-4 district fact sheets) plus the Census-2011 parent of each NFHS-5 district that has no NFHS-4 value (transition `4to5`). This is the only way to add the 132 districts that changed between NFHS-4 and NFHS-5 to the training data.
